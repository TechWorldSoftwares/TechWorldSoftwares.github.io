FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my data ever uploaded anywhere? ▼

No. TECH-World Data Recovery runs entirely on your PC. Nothing you scan or recover is ever sent over the network — verified in the app's own source code.

What's the difference between Free and Premium? ▼

Free lets you scan disk images and save individual files up to 500MB per session. Premium removes that cap and unlocks physical drive scanning, batch recovery, Forensic Mode, Duplicate Cleaner, Database Viewer, and Android Backup.

Can I recover deleted files from my Android phone? ▼

Only accessible (not-yet-deleted) files can be pulled from a non-rooted phone — Android's security model blocks raw storage access even for us. See the Android product page for what's realistically possible.

How do I get a license? ▼

Email us at techworld.official@outlook.com with your chosen plan from the Pricing page, and we'll issue your license key directly.

Does my license work on multiple PCs? ▼

Each license key is tied to you as a customer, not to a specific machine — reach out if you need it on more than one device.

What happens when my license expires? ▼

The app automatically drops back to the Free tier — your recovered files stay exactly where you saved them; nothing is deleted.
