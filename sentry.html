// Technical direction

Built for scalable surveillance workflows.

Platform: Windows 10/11 64-bit with IP camera, ONVIF, Hikvision and Dahua support planned in the product specification.

Video: H.264 / H.265. Channels scale from 4 to 32, with LAN, WAN and P2P connectivity concepts.

Storage: 1TB–20TB HDD support, auto-overwrite and optional cloud backup workflows.

Use cases: Shops, offices, warehouses, schools, hospitals, parking areas and factories.
